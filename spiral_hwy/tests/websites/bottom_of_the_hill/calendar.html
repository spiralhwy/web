After 35 legendary years, the original cast of Bottom of the Hill is taking one final bow! The venue isn't done rockin', but this marks the end of our chapter as owners. To celebrate, Ramona Downey, Kathleen Owen, and Lynn Schwarz are throwing a weeklong bash —in the last week of the year— to honor the bands, loyal fans, friends, family, and colleagues who built this temple to music with us.

Saturday and Sunday nights (12/26 and 12/27) will be a two-night blowout featuring packed, short-set performances from our favorite Bay Area bands. Low ticket prices, huge local talent.
Monday and Tuesday (12/28 and 12/29) feature high-octane surf/punk bands Man or Astro-Man? and The Subsonics.
Wednesday and Thursday (12/30 and 12/31) bring soulful local powerhouse Shannon Shaw to close out the year, joined by Tino Drima on Wednesday and Will Sprott on NYE.

Join us any or all nights! Enjoy the bands and DJs, have a drink with us, and grab a free goodbye poster by local artist Emily Fromm!
